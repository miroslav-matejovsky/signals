# Experimentation Principle

During the experimentation phase, development is driven by learning rather than stability.
Solutions are built to validate assumptions, explore architectural options, and reduce uncertainty as quickly as possible.
Backward compatibility is not maintained, code is considered disposable, and designs, APIs,
and data models may be changed or replaced without migration requirements.
Production concerns and non-functional requirements are intentionally deferred until the target architecture,
domain boundaries, and business hypotheses have been validated.
Success is measured by evidence gained, assumptions eliminated, and architectural clarity achieved.
